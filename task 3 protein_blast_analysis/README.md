# Task 3: Protein BLAST Analysis

## Objective
To perform a BLASTP search using a protein sequence and analyze the top three hits.

## Query Sequence
Protein: Angiotensin-converting enzyme (ACE)

## BLAST Settings
- Program: BLASTP
- Database: Non-redundant protein sequences (nr)
- Organism filter: None

## Top 3 Hits

| Rank | Organism | Query Cover | Percent Identity | E-value |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Homo sapiens | 100% | 100.00% | 0.0 |
| 2 | Homo sapiens | 100% | 99.62% | 0.0 |
| 3 | Pan troglodytes | 100% | 99.31% | 0.0 |

## Summary

The first BLASTP hit showed a 100% sequence identity and 100% query coverage, confirming an exact match to the ACE query sequence.

The second hit also originated from Homo sapiens and showed 99.62% sequence identity, indicating a highly similar ACE-related protein sequence.

The third hit from Pan troglodytes showed 99.31% sequence identity and 100% query coverage. This demonstrates that the ACE protein is highly conserved between closely related primates.

All three hits had an E-value of 0.0, indicating highly significant sequence similarity.

## Conclusion

The BLASTP analysis showed that the ACE protein is highly conserved, particularly among closely related primates.
